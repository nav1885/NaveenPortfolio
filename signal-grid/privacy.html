Privacy Policy — Signal Grid

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Signal Grid is a watch face for Garmin Connect IQ devices. Your privacy is simple to explain because Signal Grid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What Signal Grid accesses

To display information on the watch face, Signal Grid reads data on the device only:

  • Activity and health metrics already recorded by your Garmin watch — heart rate, resting heart rate, body battery, VO₂ Max, steps, floors, active minutes, stress, calories, distance, respiration, pulse ox, and altitude.
  • Local weather conditions synced to your watch, used solely to show sunrise/sunset times.
  • Your on-device settings for this watch face (accent color, time format, and which metrics appear in each slot).

What Signal Grid does NOT do

  • It does not transmit any data off your watch. There is no server, no backend, and no network connection made by this watch face.
  • It does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • It does not require an account or login.

All processing happens locally on your Garmin device to render the watch face. The metric values shown are provided by the device's own sensors and Garmin's platform APIs, and are never sent anywhere by Signal Grid.

Data retention

Signal Grid retains no data. It only reads current values to draw the screen; nothing is persisted by the app beyond your watch-face configuration settings, which remain on your device and in your Garmin account settings as managed by Garmin.

Children's privacy

Signal Grid does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
