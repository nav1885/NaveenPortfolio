I'm an engineering manager at Amazon, leading the core platform team for Rufus, the AI shopping assistant. The platform is the foundation for building and hosting its agents and skills — routing, planning, orchestration — to answer a customer query accurately and reliably at scale.

I studied computer science at Bangalore Institute of Technology and Florida International University, and I've spent the last 13 years at Amazon — Lab126, Halo, Last Mile, and now Rufus — wearing a different hat at each, on problems that had little in common.

I like chasing hard problems, and there's a real joy in solving them. The side projects are that same itch on my own time: I build them end to end, usually to fix something that was bugging me.

When I'm not working or tinkering with a side project, I'm on a bike — mountain and road both. Weekends usually mean the local trails, the Santa Cruz mountains, or the long, winding roads south of Almaden Valley. It's no accident two of those projects, VoxRider and Veloscape, started right there in the saddle.
